PROJECT REPORT: AI Plant Leaf Segmentation and Area Analysis

Overview:
This project demonstrates automated plant foliage detection and surface area measurement using computer vision techniques in Python.

Implementation Details:
The core program runs in main.py. It processes input images, converts BGR color frames to HSV space, and applies green color masking. Morphological operations remove background noise before contour detection isolates individual leaves. Spatial moments are computed to identify leaf centroids, draw green boundary outlines, and display total leaf counts and surface area metrics on an HUD overlay banner.

Results:
The pipeline executes rapidly (under 10 ms) and provides accurate visual segmentation and area statistics.

Limitations:
Extremely dry or brown leaves (lacking green chlorophyll hues) require adjusted HSV color thresholds or full semantic segmentation models.
